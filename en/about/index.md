Hello. I am someone trying to own less, use things longer, and give more time and attention to what matters.

This is not a guide to becoming a perfect minimalist. It is closer to a small living experiment—a record of what I bring in, what I keep, and what I let go.

## What I write about

- Lessons from reducing possessions and everyday consumption
- Things I currently use or have finished using
- Purchases I considered and ultimately decided against
- The process of choosing one thing and using it well

For each item, I document why I considered it, how I chose it, and what using it was actually like. When a purchase link appears, it is reference information for that decision rather than an unconditional recommendation.

## Why this blog exists

To me, minimal living is not simply about owning fewer things. It is about understanding what supports my life, being able to explain my choices, and making full use of what I already have.

I hope these notes offer a useful point of reference to anyone asking similar questions.
