## Why I Needed It

I wanted cookware that would last instead of replacing a coated pan every few years. It also needed to move between the stovetop and oven.

## Why I Chose This Model

The 10.25-inch size was large enough for one or two servings while still fitting my storage. It arrived seasoned, and its one-piece construction leaves almost nothing to break.

### Other Models I Considered

- Staub Cast Iron Fry Pan 26cm — refined finish, but costly and heavy.
- Victoria 10-inch Skillet — similar value, but a less comfortable handle.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

With proper preheating, eggs and meat release well and develop a strong sear. It is heavy and must be dried immediately, but the care routine quickly became automatic.
