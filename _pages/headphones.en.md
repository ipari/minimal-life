## Why I Needed It

I needed desk headphones for music and video editing without monitoring a battery. Replaceable cables and ear pads were important for long-term use.

## Why I Chose This Model

The sound was not overly colored, the cups fold for storage, and replacement parts are easy to find. A detachable cable also reduced the most likely point of failure.

### Other Models I Considered

- Sony MDR-7506 — lighter, but with a fixed cable.
- Beyerdynamic DT 770 Pro — comfortable, but less portable and harder to drive.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

Isolation is sufficient and fine details are easy to hear while working. Clamp force softened with use, though the pads still become warm during long sessions.
