## Why I Needed It

I wanted to brew one cup at home while reducing capsule and packaging waste. The method also had to remain simple and electricity-free.

## Why I Chose This Model

The filter and dripper are uncomplicated, and replacement papers are widely available. It offers enough control to learn with while remaining useful long term.

### Other Models I Considered

- Kalita Wave 185 — consistent extraction, but pricier proprietary filters.
- Chemex 3-Cup — beautiful, but more demanding filters and glass care.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

Results varied with my pouring at first, but became reliable after settling on a base recipe. There are almost no parts to clean, so it is easy to use every day.
