## Why I Needed It

I needed one winter coat for both work and more formal occasions, with a shape that would remain useful for many years.

## Why I Chose This Model

The plain charcoal Chester style works with existing shirts and knitwear. Its cashmere blend also felt softer and lighter than similarly thick all-wool options.

### Other Models I Considered

- COS Tailored Wool Coat — good silhouette, but heavy with overly long sleeves.
- Mango Wool Blend Coat — less expensive, but noticeably rougher fabric.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

It works with both tailoring and jeans, eliminating the need for separate winter coats. It is light for severe cold, but layering a thin insulated jacket handles most Seoul winter days.
