## Why I Needed It

I wanted to stop unlocking my phone—and seeing every notification—whenever I checked the time. The clock also needed to be quiet and readable across the room.

## Why I Chose This Model

High-contrast markings are easy to read at a distance, and the simple form works with the rest of the room. A nearly silent movement made it suitable near the bedroom.

### Other Models I Considered

- MUJI Analog Wall Clock — minimal, but the numerals looked small from afar.
- Braun BC17 — highly legible, but I preferred a less plastic finish.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

A quick glance now replaces reaching for my phone. It is nearly inaudible at night and runs long enough on one battery that maintenance is rare.
