## Why I Needed It

I needed a way to write longer text on a tablet or phone without carrying a laptop. It had to stay clearly lighter and thinner than my main keyboard.

## Why I Chose This Model

The integrated cover protects the keys and device switching is quick. It occupied less bag space and weighed less than the alternatives.

### Other Models I Considered

- Apple Magic Keyboard — familiar feel, but difficult to protect in transit.
- Logitech K380 — affordable and reliable, but thicker and heavier.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

It is stable enough for short mobile work and rarely needs battery attention. The narrow spacing caused mistakes at first, but after a few days longer notes became comfortable.
