## Why I Needed It

I wanted to buy fewer disposable water bottles while out. My everyday bottle needed to be light enough for a bag and simple to clean.

## Why I Chose This Model

The 532 ml size balanced weight and capacity, while single-wall stainless steel stayed light and resisted odors. A simple, replaceable cap also mattered.

### Other Models I Considered

- Nalgene Wide Mouth 500ml — lighter, but I noticed a plastic smell.
- Hydro Flask Standard Mouth 18oz — better insulation, but heavier and wider.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

The broad opening is easy to clean even without a brush, and water keeps a neutral taste. Cold drinks create condensation outside, an expected tradeoff for prioritizing low weight over insulation.
