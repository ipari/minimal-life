On New Year's Day, I decided to begin living more minimally. Looking at everything accumulated over the years, I asked what I truly needed and what actually made me happy.

> Minimalism is not owning little; it is keeping only what truly matters.

I will record what I release, what I keep, and what I learn along the way.
