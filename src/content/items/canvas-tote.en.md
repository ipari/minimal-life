## Why I Needed It

I needed a light reusable bag for groceries and short errands, replacing disposable shopping bags. It also had to fold down small when not in use.

## Why I Chose This Model

The medium size fit groceries and daily essentials without becoming oversized. The firm, nearly logo-free canvas stands upright and feels durable.

### Other Models I Considered

- L.L.Bean Boat and Tote Small — sturdier, but heavier and more rigid.
- Baggu Duck Bag — good storage, but the straps felt too long.

## What It Has Been Like to Use

The wide handles stay comfortable with a heavier load and the flat base is stable. The light fabric shows marks, but it returns to shape well after washing.
