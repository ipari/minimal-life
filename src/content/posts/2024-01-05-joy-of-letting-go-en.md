Today I sorted my wardrobe and donated clothes I had not worn for over a year. At first it felt wasteful, but the empty space made both the closet and my mind feel lighter.

## What I learned

- Most future “somedays” never arrive.
- Memories remain even when objects leave.
- Open space creates room in the mind.
